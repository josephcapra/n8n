Frequently Asked Questions

How many new construction communities are in Florida?
Paradise Realty FLA tracks over 988 active new construction communities across 37 Florida counties, ranging from affordable homes under $300K to ultra-luxury estates over $7 million.
Which Florida counties have the most new construction?
The counties with the most new construction communities include Lake County (98), Orange County (77), Palm Beach County (72), Osceola County (71), and St. Lucie County (53).
What builders are active in Florida new construction?
Major national builders active in Florida include Lennar, D.R. Horton, Pulte Homes, Toll Brothers, Taylor Morrison, KB Home, K. Hovnanian, GL Homes, and Kolter Homes, along with numerous regional builders.
How do I find new construction with builder incentives?
Paradise Realty tracks current builder incentives including closing cost assistance, rate buydowns, and design center credits. Contact us at (772) 247-7110 for communities with active incentive programs.
