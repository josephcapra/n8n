About Sawgrass Lakes

West Melbourne · Brevard County, Florida

Sawgrass Lakes is D.R. Horton's flagship master-planned community in West Melbourne, Brevard County — a large-scale development built around two freshwater lakes with nearly 1,000 homes across multiple distinct neighborhoods. Active new construction is currently available in The Landings, starting from the low $300s. The community is anchored by a 9.5-acre resort recreation complex and 8,000 sq ft Grand Clubhouse — an amenity campus that rivals resort communities at significantly higher price points.

Sawgrass Lakes is a large-scale master-planned community developed by D.R. Horton across multiple distinct neighborhoods — The Landings (currently active new construction), The Meadows, The Sanctuary (premium lakefront), The Preserves, and The Savannahs (a 55+ gated section). This multi-phase structure means buyers have genuine choice within a single community address: resale in established sections, new construction in The Landings, and premium lakefront lots in The Sanctuary. A local specialist can walk you through the current availability across all active phases.

The community's 9.5-acre recreation complex is a genuine differentiator at this price point — the lagoon-style pool, clubhouse, pickleball, tennis, volleyball, outdoor movie area, and lakefront pier collectively create a resort lifestyle that most communities below the $400k mark cannot match. Two large freshwater lakes run through the community, providing waterfront lot options throughout the neighborhood and a scenic setting that makes Sawgrass Lakes one of West Melbourne's most visually distinctive new construction addresses.

Why Choose Sawgrass Lakes?

Key reasons buyers are choosing this West Melbourne master-planned community

9.5-Acre Resort Recreation Complex

The Club at Sawgrass Lakes' 8,000 sq ft Grand Clubhouse plus 9.5-acre outdoor amenity campus — resort pool, pickleball, tennis, sand volleyball, fire pit, outdoor movie area, and lakefront pier — is a rare amenity package at the low-to-mid $300s price range.

Multi-Phase Master Plan

With multiple sub-neighborhoods at different price points — The Landings (new construction from low $300s), The Sanctuary (premium lakefront), and The Savannahs (55+ gated section) — Sawgrass Lakes can accommodate a wide range of buyers within a single cohesive community.

Lakefront Setting Throughout

Two large freshwater lakes and numerous smaller ponds run through the community. Many lots offer direct water views or waterfront frontage — a lifestyle amenity that commands a premium in most Florida communities but is naturally integrated into Sawgrass Lakes' layout.

West Melbourne Location

Positioned between Melbourne and Palm Bay off Minton Road with easy I-95 access, Sawgrass Lakes puts residents within 20 minutes of Melbourne's major aerospace employers (L3Harris, Northrop Grumman), the Indian River Lagoon, and Melbourne's expanding retail and dining corridor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Sawgrass Lakes and buying new construction in Brevard County

Where exactly is Sawgrass Lakes located?
Sawgrass Lakes is located in West Melbourne, Brevard County — not Palm Bay. The community sits off Henry Ave in West Melbourne, providing convenient access to both Melbourne's employment corridor along US-1 and Wickham Rd, and I-95 for commuters. Major employers including L3Harris Technologies and Embraer are within 15–20 minutes.
What is the starting price at Sawgrass Lakes?
Sawgrass Lakes by D.R. Horton starts in the low $300s. The active phase — Sawgrass Lakes: The Landings — features single-family homes ranging from approximately 1,641 to 3,408 square feet. D.R. Horton's standard 'America's Builder' inclusions keep entry pricing competitive for a gated community in this location.
Is Sawgrass Lakes gated?
Yes. Sawgrass Lakes is a gated community with a controlled-access entrance. Residents access through the main gate. The community's 9.5-acre recreation complex is private to residents and includes the Club at Sawgrass Lakes amenity campus.
What amenities does Sawgrass Lakes offer?
Sawgrass Lakes features a 9.5-acre resort-style recreation complex anchored by the Club at Sawgrass Lakes. Amenities include a lagoon pool, pickleball courts, tennis courts, sand volleyball, outdoor movie area, fire pit, two playgrounds, a yoga studio, fitness center, business center, and a lakefront pier — one of the most complete amenity packages among new construction communities at this price point in West Melbourne.
