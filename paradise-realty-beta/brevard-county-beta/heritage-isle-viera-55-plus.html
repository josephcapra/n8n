About Heritage Isle at Viera

Viera · Brevard County, Florida

HOPA Age-Restricted Community: Heritage Isle is certified under the Housing for Older Persons Act (HOPA). At least one resident per household must be 55 years of age or older. This designation ensures a community of like-minded active adults and maintains the community's age-qualified status long-term.

Heritage Isle is DiVosta's established 55+ community within Viera — offering gated active-adult living with a private 18-hole executive golf course. Known for its robust social programs and high owner-occupancy rate, Heritage Isle represents the premium choice for buyers seeking an age-qualified community within Brevard County's most complete master-planned address.

Built by DiVosta, a PulteGroup brand with deep expertise in Florida active-adult development, Heritage Isle benefits from PulteGroup's construction quality standards combined with DiVosta's proven 55+ lifestyle programming. Homes range from 1,200 to 2,200 square feet with 2 to 3 bedrooms — well-sized for active adults who value comfort without the maintenance burden of larger homes.

The HOA fee of approximately $380 per month includes lawn care and access to the community's full amenity campus — including the executive golf course, resort pool, tennis, pickleball, fitness center, and an active social director who coordinates the community's programming calendar.
