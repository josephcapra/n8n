About Viera

Viera · Brevard County, Florida

Viera is Brevard County's premier master-planned community — spanning thousands of acres west of Melbourne along I-95. Multiple national builders offer new construction across diverse price points, from attainable townhomes to custom estate homes. Close to Kennedy Space Center, Patrick Space Force Base, and Brevard County's renowned Atlantic beaches, Viera represents one of Florida's most complete planned communities.

Active builders within Viera include Meritage Homes, DiVosta, K. Hovnanian, and Dream Finders — each offering distinct floorplans, price points, and neighborhood settings within the larger master plan. This multi-builder environment means buyers have genuine choice, but it also requires expertise to navigate effectively.

With multiple builders and neighborhoods, navigating Viera requires expert guidance. Our local specialist knows which neighborhoods have current inventory and the best value propositions — saving buyers significant time and ensuring they make an informed decision in a complex, multi-builder market.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Viera Master-Planned Community and buying new construction in Brevard County

Who are the builders currently active in Viera?
Viera's master plan supports multiple builders simultaneously. Active or recently active builders include Meritage Homes, K. Hovnanian, Dream Finders Homes, and DiVosta (PulteGroup). Each builder brings distinct price points, floor plan styles, and included features. A local specialist matched through Paradise Realty FLA can walk you through current availability across all active builders in one consultation.
What is the starting price for new construction in Viera?
New construction in Viera starts in the mid $300s, with pricing varying by builder, community, and home series. Entry-level attached and paired-villa product can be found under $400k; larger single-family homes in the $400k–$600k+ range are common. Heritage Isle at Viera (55+) starts in the low $300s for resale inventory.
Is Viera a good long-term investment?
Viera consistently ranks as one of Florida's best-planned communities. The master plan's infrastructure investment — road network, retail, medical, schools, parks, and wetland preservation — underpins long-term value. Proximity to the Space Coast's growing aerospace and defense employment base (L3Harris, Northrop Grumman, SpaceX, NASA) adds economic resilience. Appreciation in Viera has historically tracked above Brevard County averages.
Are there 55+ communities within the Viera master plan?
Yes — Heritage Isle at Viera is DiVosta's HOPA-certified 55+ community within the Viera master plan. It features a private 18-hole executive golf course and robust amenity campus. At least one resident per household must be 55 or older to qualify. Other Viera neighborhoods are all-ages communities.
