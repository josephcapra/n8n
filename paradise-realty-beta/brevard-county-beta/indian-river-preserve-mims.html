Does Indian River Preserve have a golf course?
Yes — Indian River Preserve in Mims is built around a golf course community, offering residents access to on-site golf. This is a distinguishing feature for D.R. Horton new construction at the mid $300s price point — golf-adjacent new construction at that price is uncommon in Brevard County.
What is the starting price at Indian River Preserve?
Indian River Preserve by D.R. Horton starts in the mid $300s. The golf course community setting and north Brevard location near Kennedy Space Center provide strong value relative to comparable communities in Palm Bay and Melbourne at higher price points.
How far is Indian River Preserve from Kennedy Space Center?
Indian River Preserve in Mims is approximately 20–25 minutes from Kennedy Space Center on Merritt Island. Indian River Preserve is not a fully gated community, though its golf course setting provides a sense of enclosure and community identity. Buyers seeking gated new construction in north Brevard should compare Fawn Lake in Mims, which offers a gated option at a higher price point.
