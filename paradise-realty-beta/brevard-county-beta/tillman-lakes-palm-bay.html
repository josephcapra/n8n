Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Tillman Lakes and buying new construction in Brevard County

Does Tillman Lakes have a boat launch?
Yes — Tillman Lakes features a private community boat launch, one of the most distinctive amenity features among Palm Bay new construction communities at this price point. Residents have access to the launch for kayaks, canoes, and small watercraft — providing direct access to the St. Johns River tributary system and, ultimately, the Indian River Lagoon.
What is the starting price at Tillman Lakes?
Tillman Lakes by Lennar in Palm Bay starts in the low $300s. Lennar's 'Everything's Included®' model delivers smart home features, upgraded finishes, and energy-efficient construction as standard. The private boat launch adds a premium amenity at an attainable price point.
Is Tillman Lakes close to the water?
Yes — Tillman Lakes sits adjacent to Tillman Canal and has direct water access via the community's private boat launch. This is one of very few new construction Palm Bay communities with functional watercraft access built into the community infrastructure at the low $300s price tier.
What amenities are available at Tillman Lakes?
Tillman Lakes residents enjoy Lennar's community amenities including a resort-style pool, clubhouse, and fitness facilities, plus the community's defining private boat launch. Lennar's 'Everything's Included®' model provides smart home technology and upgraded interior features at no added cost.
