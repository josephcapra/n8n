About St. Johns Preserve

Palm Bay · Brevard County, Florida

St. Johns Preserve is a new construction community in Palm Bay, Brevard County — Landsea Homes single-family detached and townhome community in Palm Bay near St. Johns Heritage Parkway. Pricing starts from Low $300s as of May 2026.

Landsea Homes is a builder distinguished by its focus on high-performance home construction — better insulation, energy efficiency systems, and healthier indoor air quality than conventional production building. For buyers who prioritize long-term operating costs alongside purchase price, Landsea's Net Zero Ready standards translate to meaningfully lower utility bills.

St. Johns Preserve's location near St. Johns Heritage Parkway gives buyers practical access to Palm Bay's growing commercial and retail corridor, while the preserve-adjacent setting provides natural visual buffers uncommon in dense suburban subdivisions. The dual product offering — detached single-family homes and attached townhomes — gives buyers choice within a single community, useful for comparing ownership costs across product types.

Why Choose St. Johns Preserve?

Key reasons buyers are choosing this Palm Bay community

High-Performance Home Construction

Landsea Homes builds to standards that exceed typical production builder energy codes — better insulation, smarter HVAC sizing, and materials that reduce operating costs over the life of the home. In Florida's hot climate, that translates to real annual savings.

Townhome Option Available

St. Johns Preserve offers both detached single-family homes and attached townhomes — a product mix that allows buyers to compare ownership structures, HOA obligations, and price points within a single community before deciding.

Preserve Setting

The preserve-adjacent location buffers St. Johns residents from typical suburban density and provides natural views and wildlife corridors that are genuinely difficult to find in new construction communities at this price point.

Location & Area

Nearby: Melbourne employment centers (~20 min north), Indian River Lagoon (~10 min east), I-95 interchange, Vero Beach (~30 min south), Sebastian (~25 min south), Kennedy Space Center (~35 min north.

Brevard County Context: Brevard County is Florida's Space Coast — anchored by Kennedy Space Center and a growing commercial aerospace employment base. With Atlantic Ocean beaches, Indian River Lagoon access, and a year-round outdoor lifestyle, Brevard offers an authentic coastal Florida character at price points below most South Florida and Treasure Coast markets.

Commute Patterns: Palm Bay buyers typically commute toward Melbourne, the Cape Canaveral/Kennedy Space Center corridor, and in some cases the Orlando metro via SR-528. A local specialist can advise on commute times from specific lot positions within the community.
