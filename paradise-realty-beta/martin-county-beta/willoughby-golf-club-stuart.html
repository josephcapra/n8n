About Willoughby Golf Club

Stuart · Martin County, Florida · Stuart's Premier Luxury Golf Address

Willoughby Golf Club is Stuart's premier luxury golf and country club community — an established 24-hour guard-gated enclave where championship golf, resort-style amenities, and custom estate living come together in the heart of Martin County. With homes ranging from 2,500 to over 6,000 square feet, Willoughby attracts discerning buyers who refuse to compromise on lifestyle or location.

The community's championship 18-hole golf course is complemented by a full country club campus: tennis and pickleball courts, a resort-style pool, fitness center, and a clubhouse with dining — all within the gates of one of Martin County's most sought-after zip codes.

New homesites occasionally become available for custom construction within Willoughby Golf Club. Paradise Realty FLA can identify current availability and help you understand the community's builder approval and architectural review process — ensuring that buyers who want to build on a specific lot have the right guidance from the outset.

Willoughby's location places residents close to downtown Stuart's waterfront district and the Marriott Hutchinson Island Beach & Marina resort, with the St. Lucie River just minutes away. For buyers seeking luxury without the congestion of Palm Beach County, Willoughby Golf Club represents Stuart's finest address.

Why Choose Willoughby Golf Club?

What defines this community as Stuart's prestige golf address

Championship Golf Lifestyle

An 18-hole championship course maintained to private club standards — with golf, tennis, pickleball, and a full clubhouse campus included in your community membership.

Stuart's Prestige Address

Willoughby Golf Club carries one of Martin County's most recognized addresses. Within the gates of this 24-hour guarded community, estate homes sit on generous lots with golf and waterway views.

Custom Build Opportunity

Select homesites within Willoughby allow buyers to bring their own custom builder — creating a truly bespoke estate in one of Stuart's most established communities. Paradise Realty FLA can identify available lots.

Comprehensive Country Club

The HOA of approximately $800 per month covers a full country club amenity package — golf, tennis, pickleball, pool, fitness, and dining — representing exceptional value at the luxury tier.

Waterway & Golf Views

Many homesites and residences enjoy views of the golf course fairways or the community's internal waterways — the kind of setting that defines the Treasure Coast luxury lifestyle.

Location & Area

Nearby: Downtown Stuart waterfront (~10 min), Marriott Hutchinson Island Beach & Marina, St. Lucie River, Cleveland Clinic Martin County, Hutchinson Island beaches, I-95 access.

School District: Martin County School District — verify current zoning by specific lot address with the district.

Stuart's central location within Martin County places Willoughby Golf Club residents equidistant from the Atlantic coast beaches and the peaceful interior waterways of the St. Lucie River basin. Martin County's strict growth management policies have preserved a quality of life here that is increasingly difficult to find in coastal Florida — making Willoughby Golf Club not just a great community, but a sound long-term investment in a market with constrained supply.
