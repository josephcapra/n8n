About Seagate at Hutchinson Shores

Jensen Beach · Martin County, Florida · Hutchinson Island Area

Seagate at Hutchinson Shores offers luxury new construction in one of Martin County's most desirable coastal locations — positioned close to the Atlantic Ocean and the Indian River Lagoon near Jensen Beach. GHO Homes brings the same quality found in their well-regarded Indian River County communities to this exceptional Martin County location.

Homes range from 2,800 to 5,500 square feet with 3 to 5 bedrooms and 2 to 3-car garages — thoughtfully designed for buyers who demand space, quality, and a coastal lifestyle without compromise. The HOA of approximately $450 per month covers community amenities, landscaping, and gate operations.

The community's setting near Hutchinson Island places residents within minutes of Atlantic Ocean beaches, the Indian River Lagoon, and the charming town center of Jensen Beach. Nearby boat ramp access and the community's intracoastal and ocean proximity make Seagate at Hutchinson Shores a natural choice for boating and fishing enthusiasts.

For buyers familiar with GHO Homes' work in Vero Beach — communities like Lucaya Pointe and Bent Pine Preserve — Seagate at Hutchinson Shores delivers the same hallmark of quality construction and thoughtful design, now in one of Martin County's most coveted coastal corridors.

GHO Homes Incentives & Availability

GHO Homes periodically offers incentives on available inventory homes and new construction starts. Availability and pricing change frequently as the community builds out.

Why Choose Seagate at Hutchinson Shores?

What makes this community exceptional in Martin County's coastal market

Hutchinson Island Coastal Lifestyle

Hutchinson Island's barrier island beaches are among the cleanest and least crowded on the entire Florida east coast. Seagate residents enjoy a short drive to world-class swimming, surfing, and waterfront dining.

GHO Homes Luxury Quality

GHO Homes has built a strong reputation for luxury construction quality across the Treasure Coast. Their commitment to craftsmanship, thoughtful floorplans, and finish quality is well-established in the region.

Intracoastal & Ocean Proximity

The Indian River Lagoon to the west and the Atlantic Ocean to the east — Seagate at Hutchinson Shores puts residents at the center of one of Florida's most remarkable natural coastal environments.

Jensen Beach Location

Jensen Beach offers the authentic Florida coastal town experience — locally owned restaurants, a laid-back downtown, and easy access to Stuart, Port Salerno, and the broader Martin County area.

Gated Waterfront Community

A gated community with resort amenities, HOA of approximately $450/month, and the security and privacy that luxury buyers expect — at a price point below comparable Palm Beach County options.

Location & Area

Nearby: Hutchinson Island Atlantic beaches, Indian River Lagoon, Jensen Beach Causeway, downtown Jensen Beach, downtown Stuart (~15 min), Cleveland Clinic Martin County, boat ramps & marinas, US-1 corridor.

School District: Martin County School District — verify current zoning by specific lot address with the district.

Jensen Beach occupies a unique position in Martin County — just inland from the Hutchinson Island barrier island, with the Indian River Lagoon separating the town from the beach. This geography gives residents dual access: ocean beaches to the east and the calm, fish-rich waters of the Lagoon to the west. It is one of Florida's truly special coastal living environments, and new gated luxury construction here is rare.
