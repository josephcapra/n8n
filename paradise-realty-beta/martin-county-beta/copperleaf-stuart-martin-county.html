About Copperleaf

Stuart · Martin County, Florida

Copperleaf is D.R. Horton's attainable gated new construction community in Stuart — offering Treasure Coast buyers the rare combination of gated security, a resort-style amenity package, and a price point well below the county's luxury median. All-block construction, modern floorplans, and a central Stuart location make this one of Martin County's most compelling new construction values.

Homes range from 1,700 to 3,100 square feet with 3 to 5 bedrooms and 2 to 3-car garages — a versatile lineup suited to first-time buyers, growing families, and relocating professionals alike. HOA fees of approximately $180 per month cover community amenities and common area maintenance.

Situated near US-1 in Stuart, residents enjoy quick access to downtown Stuart's vibrant waterfront district, Sailfish Splash Waterpark, and Martin Memorial Hospital (Cleveland Clinic Martin County). Hutchinson Island beaches are a short drive east, and the community's preserve and lake views create a peaceful natural backdrop that's hard to find at this price point.

D.R. Horton Closing Cost Incentives

D.R. Horton regularly offers closing cost incentives and rate buydown programs when buyers use their preferred lender. These incentives can be substantial — but they are only accessible when you have registered buyer representation.
