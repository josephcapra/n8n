About Harbour Ridge Yacht & Country Club

Port Salerno · Martin County, Florida · On the St. Lucie River

HOPA-Certified 55+ Community — Harbour Ridge Yacht & Country Club is an age-restricted community under the Housing for Older Persons Act (HOPA). At least one resident per household must be 55 years of age or older. Contact Paradise Realty FLA for full qualification details.

Harbour Ridge Yacht & Country Club is one of Florida's premier private waterfront communities — situated along the St. Lucie River in Port Salerno, Martin County. With two championship 18-hole golf courses, a deep-water marina, world-class tennis facilities, and a clubhouse with fine dining, Harbour Ridge offers an amenity campus that rivals anything in the state.

The community offers a range of residential options including single family homes and condominiums, with both resale inventory and new construction opportunities available within its exceptional setting. Prices vary widely depending on location within the community, home type, and condition — starting around mid $400s and extending well above that for larger waterfront properties.

Harbour Ridge accepts new construction within certain sections of the community. Paradise Realty FLA can identify available homesites and walk you through the community's approval and purchasing process — ensuring you have expert representation at every stage.

HOA fees of approximately $2,100–$2,500 per month are comprehensive — covering golf privileges on both courses, marina access and boat docks, all fitness and recreational facilities, clubhouse dining, 24-hour security, and community maintenance. For buyers seeking a true all-inclusive luxury lifestyle, the value proposition is compelling.

Navigating the Harbour Ridge Purchase Process

Harbour Ridge has a community approval process and specific procedures for both resale and new construction purchases. Working with a knowledgeable local agent is essential to a smooth transaction.

Important: Contact Paradise Realty FLA before visiting the community or contacting any on-site sales staff to ensure your buyer representation rights are properly established.

Why Choose Harbour Ridge?

What makes this community stand apart in Martin County

Private Waterfront & Marina

A deep-water marina on the St. Lucie River gives boating residents direct access to the Atlantic Ocean and the Indian River Lagoon — a rare and coveted amenity in any private community.

Two Championship Golf Courses

Two distinct 18-hole courses ensure tee times are always available. Both courses are maintained to private club standards with panoramic river and preserve views throughout.

World-Class Amenities

From tennis and pickleball to croquet, multiple pools, a fitness center, and fine dining in the clubhouse — Harbour Ridge's monthly fees cover a lifestyle that is difficult to replicate elsewhere in Martin County.

55+ Active Lifestyle

A HOPA-certified community designed for active adults who want to be surrounded by like-minded neighbors — the calendar of social and recreational events is one of the most robust on the Treasure Coast.
