About Eden Crest

Minneola · Lake County, Florida

Eden Crest is a Lennar community — and Lennar's 'Everything's Included®' program sets it apart from competitors: appliances, smart home features, window blinds, and premium finishes are included in the base price. There's no separate design studio invoice that surprises buyers at contract. What you see in the model is what you get at the base price — a transparent value proposition that helps buyers compare total cost of ownership accurately.

Lennar's product tiers span Essential through Luxury and Estate, with NextGen® floor plans (a separate in-law suite with private entrance) available in select communities. Lennar is also one of Florida's most active master-plan developers, often controlling the underlying land in its communities — reducing third-party risk for buyers and giving Lennar more flexibility on phasing and pricing.

Eden Crest is located in Minneola, Lake County — The Florida Turnpike and US-27 connect Lake County to the Orange County employment corridor. Clermont to downtown Orlando runs approximately 35–45 minutes off-peak; Minneola to I-4 via SR-429 is about 25 minutes. The area's employment and lifestyle context makes Minneola a practical choice for buyers who want Orlando proximity at more competitive new construction pricing.
