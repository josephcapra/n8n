About Sunbridge

St. Cloud · Osceola County, Florida

Sunbridge is a new construction HOA community in St. Cloud by D.R. Horton offering mid-range homes, currently selling. 2,500-acre community with Crystal Lagoon and resort amenities.

D.R. Horton is America's largest homebuilder by volume — a scale advantage that translates to purchasing power on materials and land, faster build cycles than smaller builders, and a national warranty program backed by a Fortune 500 company. The Express and Emerald collections extend the brand from entry-level attainable homes to premium products, with Express typically being the most aggressively priced new construction in any given market.

Location context: US-192 is Kissimmee's primary east-west corridor with Osceola Parkway (SR-522) providing fast Disney-I-4 connectivity. For Sunbridge/Narcoossee buyers, Narcoossee Road connects directly to Lake Nona and SR-417 (~30 min to MCO Airport). Kissimmee to Disney World runs ~20 min; to downtown Orlando ~30 min.
