About 400 Central

St. Petersburg · Pinellas County, Florida

400 Central is a master-planned community featuring homes from multiple builders — giving buyers the ability to compare floor plans, standard features, pricing, and warranty programs from competing homebuilders within a single master plan infrastructure. This builder-competition model typically results in more aggressive pricing and better standard specifications than single-builder alternatives at the same price point.

Buyers considering 400 Central should tour model homes from each active builder before committing — floor plan lineups, design studio options, and standard specifications vary significantly between builders even within the same price range. The community's shared infrastructure (entry, common areas, amenities) is governed by the master HOA; individual builder warranty and purchase processes differ by builder.

400 Central is located in St. Petersburg, Pinellas County — The Howard Frankland Bridge (I-275) and Gandy Bridge connect St. Petersburg to Tampa in ~25–35 min off-peak. Pinellas County's peninsular geography means the bridges are the only access points to Tampa and the mainland. The area's employment and lifestyle context makes St. Petersburg a practical choice for buyers who want Tampa proximity at more competitive new construction pricing.
