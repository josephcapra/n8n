About Parkland Golf & Country Club

Parkland · Broward County, Florida

Parkland Golf & Country Club is Toll Brothers' flagship luxury community in Broward County — positioned in Parkland, one of South Florida's most desirable suburban cities. Premium construction, golf course living, and resort amenities combine in an enclave known for excellent quality of life, top-rated schools, and a remarkably low crime rate that sets it apart from the broader South Florida market.

Homes range from 2,500 to 6,000 square feet with 3 to 6 bedrooms — a range that accommodates growing families, executive buyers, and those seeking a luxury primary residence or seasonal retreat in a world-class South Florida setting. Toll Brothers' signature attention to finishes and optional upgrades allows buyers to create a home that reflects their personal vision.

The community's 24-hour guard-gated entrance, championship golf course, and resort-style pool and spa deliver a complete country club lifestyle without leaving the neighborhood. The monthly HOA of approximately $600 covers a broad array of amenities and services that define the Parkland Golf & Country Club experience.
