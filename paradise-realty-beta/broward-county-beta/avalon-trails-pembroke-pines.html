About Avalon Trails

Pembroke Pines · Broward County, Florida

HOPA Age-Restricted Community: Avalon Trails is certified under the Housing for Older Persons Act (HOPA). At least one resident per household must be 55 years of age or older. This designation ensures a community of like-minded active adults and protects the community's age-qualified status.

Avalon Trails is GL Homes' premier 55+ community in Pembroke Pines — one of South Florida's best cities for active adult living. The community features GL Homes' signature resort lifestyle programming in the heart of Broward County's most desirable suburban area, delivering a complete active-adult experience at a price point that makes South Florida attainable.

Homes range from 1,500 to 2,600 square feet with 2 to 3 bedrooms — a well-calibrated range for empty nesters, retirees, and active adults seeking low-maintenance living without sacrificing space or comfort. The HOA fee of approximately $400 per month includes lawn care and full access to the community's resort amenity campus.

Pembroke Pines offers outstanding proximity to Memorial Hospital, Broward Health, major shopping at Pembroke Lakes Mall and Pines City Center, plus an extensive dining and entertainment scene. Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport is a short drive east — ideal for those who travel frequently or have family visiting from out of state.
