About Indian River County New Construction

Indian River County is one of Florida's most sought-after destinations for new construction — a Treasure Coast gem where Atlantic beaches, the world-renowned Indian River Lagoon, and a relaxed coastal lifestyle combine with exceptional new home value. With over 37 active communities across Vero Beach, Sebastian, Indian River Shores, and Fellsmere — built by more than 10 national and local builders — Indian River County offers the widest selection of new construction at any price point in the region.

Whether you're seeking a no-HOA affordable home starting in the low $260s or an exclusive barrier island estate above $2 million, Indian River County has a community designed for your lifestyle. Lower property taxes than Palm Beach or Broward County, world-class healthcare at Cleveland Clinic Indian River Hospital, and the authentic coastal lifestyle of the Indian River Lagoon make this county consistently appealing for buyers relocating from South Florida and out of state.

Cities & Areas — Indian River County

Vero Beach

The county seat. Home to Ocean Drive, Riverside Theatre, McKee Botanical Garden, the Indian River Lagoon, and Atlantic beaches. The majority of new construction is concentrated here.

Sebastian

A waterfront town known for Sebastian Inlet State Park — premier surf and fishing. Growing new construction market with D.R. Horton and local builders active.

Indian River Shores

An exclusive barrier island municipality between Vero Beach and the Atlantic. Home to The Strand and Seaglass by GHO Homes — some of the most prestigious new construction addresses in IRC.

Fellsmere

Florida's most authentically rural small town in IRC. Near Blue Cypress Lake. D.R. Horton offers the county's most affordable new construction here from the low $280s.

Frequently Asked Questions — Indian River County
What new construction communities are available in Indian River County in 2026?
Indian River County has 37+ active new construction communities in 2026, spanning Vero Beach, Sebastian, Indian River Shores, and Fellsmere. Builders include Meritage Homes, D.R. Horton, GHO Homes, DiVosta Homes, Toll Brothers, Maronda Homes, Century Complete, Lennar, Ryan Homes, and LifeStyle Homes. Prices range from approximately mid $200s to over $2 million.
Are there no-HOA new construction homes in Indian River County?
Yes — several communities have no HOA: Vero Lake Estates by Maronda Homes (from upper $200s), Vero Lake Estates by Century Complete (from low $300s), Sebastian Highlands by D.R. Horton (low $280s), Vero Beach Highlands by D.R. Horton, and Fellsmere scattered sites. No-HOA communities allow boats, RVs, and work vehicles without restriction.
