About Citrus Springs by Holiday Builders

Citrus Springs · Citrus County, Florida

Holiday Builders' Citrus Springs communities represent the most affordable new construction entry point in Citrus County — single-family homes starting in the low $200s in an established residential area of northern Citrus County. For first-time buyers, retirees on fixed incomes, and anyone prioritizing value per dollar over luxury amenities, the Citrus Springs Holiday Builders offering is genuinely compelling in today's Florida market.

Holiday Builders is one of Florida's most experienced regional homebuilders, known for delivering quality single-family construction at attainable price points across the state. Their Citrus Springs product is built on platted lots in the existing Citrus Springs community — buyers get a brand-new home with modern mechanicals, warranties, and energy efficiency at a price point that's difficult to match anywhere else in the county.

Location & Area

Nearby: Crystal River (~18 min west), Inverness (~22 min south), Beverly Hills (~12 min south), Dunnellon (~15 min north), Ocala (~50 min southeast).

Citrus County Context: Citrus County is Florida's Nature Coast — a region defined by spring-fed rivers, manatee sanctuaries, and some of the most accessible freshwater and Gulf recreation in the state. Citrus Springs sits in the northern part of the county, giving buyers proximity to the Crystal River National Wildlife Refuge and the Gulf while maintaining access to Citrus County's employment and healthcare corridor.

Commute Patterns: Citrus Springs residents most commonly commute toward Crystal River, Inverness, and the Lecanto/Beverly Hills employment corridor. The area is also accessible to Marion County (Ocala) for buyers employed in that market.
