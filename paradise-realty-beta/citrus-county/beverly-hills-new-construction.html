About Beverly Hills New Construction

Beverly Hills · Citrus County, Florida

Beverly Hills is one of Citrus County's most established residential areas — a large CDP in the heart of the Nature Coast where new construction from Adams Homes and regional builders starts in the low $300s. Buyers here benefit from central county access, no CDD fees on most homesites, and a quiet lifestyle that's harder to find in Florida's more expensive coastal markets.

Adams Homes is a regional Florida builder known for delivering quality single-family homes at attainable price points. Their Beverly Hills product features open floor plans, functional layouts for Florida living, and a streamlined build process. For buyers who want a brand-new home without the premium associated with larger national builders, Adams Homes represents strong value per square foot at this price tier.

Beverly Hills sits at the geographic center of Citrus County, giving buyers convenient access to Inverness, Crystal River, and the Lecanto commercial corridor. The nearby Gulf Coast, spring-fed rivers, and Withlacoochee State Forest make this an exceptional lifestyle location for buyers drawn to the outdoors, water, and a slower pace of Florida life.
