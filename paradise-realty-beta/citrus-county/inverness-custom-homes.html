About Inverness Custom Homes

Inverness · County Seat · Citrus County, Florida

Inverness is the county seat of Citrus County — and among the most desirable addresses on Florida's Nature Coast. The city sits at the western edge of the Lake Tsala Apopka chain, with Lake Henderson forming the city's scenic centerpiece. Custom home buyers here are choosing a genuinely distinct lifestyle: a walkable downtown, lake access from their neighborhood, county-seat services and infrastructure, and new construction pricing that remains well below comparable lakeside markets in Florida's more recognized corridors.

The Inverness new construction market is primarily served by custom and semi-custom builders — not the large-volume national brands. This means more design flexibility and lot selectivity for the buyer, but also a more complex process that rewards having an experienced local specialist who knows which builders are currently active, which lots are available, and how to navigate custom construction contracts that differ significantly from tract-home purchases.

Inverness benefits from its status as the county seat: HCA Florida Citrus Hospital, the Citrus County School District administration, and county government offices are all within minutes. For buyers who work locally in Citrus County's healthcare, education, or government sectors, Inverness is as close to the employment core as any community in the county — while still offering the lake access and old-Florida character that defines the Nature Coast lifestyle.

Custom home opportunities in Inverness vary by lot availability and active builder pipelines. Why Choose Inverness?

Key reasons buyers choose Citrus County's county seat

County Seat Character

Inverness has the established infrastructure, services, and civic identity of a county seat — courts, government offices, hospital, and an active downtown business district — without the congestion or cost of a larger Florida city. For buyers who want urban walkability in a small-town setting, Inverness is among the few places in Florida that delivers both.

Lake Tsala Apopka Chain Access

The Lake Tsala Apopka system is one of Central Florida's most underrated recreational assets — a chain of interconnected lakes spanning central Citrus County, home to largemouth bass fishing, kayaking, and waterfront sunsets. Inverness custom home buyers can access this system directly from their neighborhoods.

Custom Build — Your Design, Your Lot

Custom home buyers in Inverness get what volume builders can't offer: a home designed to your specifications on a lot you select. A local specialist who knows Inverness can identify available lots, connect you with proven custom builders, and guide you through the custom construction process from design through final walkthrough.

Location & Area

Nearby: Beverly Hills (~12 min west), Lecanto (~15 min northwest), Crystal River (~30 min northwest), Homosassa (~25 min southwest), Floral City (~15 min south), Ocala (~40 min southeast via US-41).

Citrus County Context: Inverness sits in the eastern interior of Citrus County — farther from the Gulf than Crystal River or Homosassa, but centrally positioned for county employment, healthcare, and services. The city has a distinct downtown walkability that none of Citrus County's other communities can match, plus the trail access of the Withlacoochee State Trail, which runs directly through town.

Commute Patterns: Inverness residents working in county healthcare (HCA Citrus Hospital), education (Citrus County School District), or county government have some of the shortest commutes in the county. Buyers commuting toward Ocala should factor in US-41 travel south; Tampa-area commuters face a longer drive and may find Sugarmill Woods or Crystal River better positioned for that pattern.

What is the Inverness new construction market in Citrus County?
Inverness is the county seat of Citrus County, FL — a lakeside community with a genuine walkable downtown, anchored by Lake Henderson and the broader Lake Tsala Apopka chain. New construction custom homes here start in the mid $300s and offer buyers county-seat character, lake access, and proximity to the county's main healthcare and government employment centers in a market where supply of true custom product remains constrained.
What lakes are near Inverness, FL?
Inverness sits directly on Lake Henderson and provides access to the Lake Tsala Apopka chain — a network of interconnected lakes and marshes spanning much of central Citrus County. Boating, fishing, kayaking, and waterfront recreation are part of daily life for Inverness residents in a way that's rarely available in similar-priced new construction markets elsewhere in Florida.
What types of builders are active in Inverness?
The Inverness new construction market is primarily served by custom and semi-custom builders rather than large-volume national builders. A local specialist familiar with active Inverness builders can guide you through current availability, lot selection, and builder contract terms specific to this market.
Is Inverness a good place to live in Citrus County?
Inverness consistently ranks among the most desirable addresses in Citrus County — the combination of walkable downtown, lake access, county seat services, and established neighborhood character is unusual in markets at this price point. The city is small by Florida standards, which is increasingly a feature rather than a limitation for buyers prioritizing quality of life over proximity to large urban centers.
