About Lecanto Custom Homes

Lecanto · County Administrative Hub · Citrus County, Florida

Lecanto occupies a unique position in Citrus County's geography and civic life — it's the unincorporated community that functions as the county's administrative and healthcare core. HCA Florida Citrus Hospital, the Citrus County School District headquarters, the county courthouse complex, and county commission offices all anchor Lecanto's central corridor. For buyers whose work, family, or lifestyle keeps them tied to Citrus County's public sector, Lecanto is the address that eliminates the commute.

New construction in Lecanto spans custom and semi-custom builders operating across established subdivisions and scattered parcels throughout the SR-44 and CR-491 corridors. Starting in the mid $300s, Lecanto buyers find product that competes on space, lot size, and design flexibility with what volume builders offer elsewhere — but with the added advantage of a location that's genuinely central to everything Citrus County offers. Beverly Hills is 10 minutes south, Inverness is 15 minutes east, Crystal River is 20 minutes west, and Pine Ridge Estates is within minutes.

Lecanto's character is defined by large lots, established neighborhoods, mature landscaping, and the kind of quiet rural feel that Citrus County buyers often move here to find. It lacks Inverness's walkable downtown and Crystal River's Gulf access — but it makes up for both with its unmatched centrality and employment proximity. For the right buyer, Lecanto is the most efficient address in Citrus County.

Lecanto's new construction market includes both subdivision lots and scattered parcels — meaning a local specialist's knowledge of active builders and available lots is especially important here. Availability changes quickly and is not centrally listed the way volume-builder inventory is.

Why Choose Lecanto?

Key reasons buyers choose Citrus County's administrative hub

On the Healthcare Campus

Lecanto is home to HCA Florida Citrus Hospital — Citrus County's main acute care facility. Healthcare workers, hospital administrators, and medical professionals can live within minutes of the hospital, eliminating the longest commute that healthcare buyers in neighboring counties accept as a given. It's a genuinely rare combination at this price point.

The Most Central Address in the County

Lecanto sits at roughly the geographic center of Citrus County — putting buyers within 20 minutes of Crystal River's Gulf access, 15 minutes of Inverness's lakeside downtown, 10 minutes of Beverly Hills, and just minutes from Pine Ridge Estates. No other Citrus County address puts all of the county's amenities this close simultaneously.

Large-Lot Character Without the Gulf Premium

Lecanto's residential areas are defined by spacious lots, mature canopy, and a rural suburban character that's hard to find at mid $300s pricing in Florida's coastal counties. Buyers who want acreage, privacy, and breathing room — without the restrictions of equestrian zoning or the price premium of Gulf-front locations — often find Lecanto hits the sweet spot.

Location & Area

Nearby: Beverly Hills (~10 min south), Inverness (~15 min east), Crystal River (~20 min west), Pine Ridge Estates (~8 min), Homosassa (~25 min southwest), Floral City (~20 min southeast).

Citrus County Context: Lecanto's position on the SR-44 corridor gives it natural cross-county access without committing buyers to either the Gulf-side west or the lake-country east. The SR-44 / CR-491 intersection is one of the county's main commercial nodes, with grocery, retail, and healthcare services nearby. For buyers who will commute in multiple directions or whose household has multiple employment destinations, Lecanto reduces total commute time for the whole family.

Commute Patterns: Lecanto residents working at HCA Citrus or in county government have the shortest possible commutes — less than 10 minutes for most. Buyers commuting to Crystal River's energy sector (Duke Energy) drive about 20–22 minutes west. The absence of a direct Interstate route means Tampa-area commuters should carefully preview the drive time, though US-19 or the Suncoast Parkway via Spring Hill are viable corridors for hybrid workers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Lecanto new construction market in Citrus County?
Lecanto is Citrus County's unincorporated government center — home to HCA Florida Citrus Hospital, the Citrus County School District, county administrative offices, and the Citrus County Courthouse complex. New construction custom and semi-custom homes here start in the mid $300s and are particularly well-positioned for buyers working within the county's major employment base, offering a central location with easy access to all corners of Citrus County.
Why is Lecanto important in Citrus County?
Lecanto functions as Citrus County's administrative and healthcare hub — it's where the county commission meets, where the main hospital operates, and where the school district administration is headquartered. This gives Lecanto a unique employment density for an unincorporated community and makes it the most centrally accessible location in the county for residents who work in any of these sectors.
What builders are active in Lecanto?
The Lecanto market is served by a mix of custom builders and regional semi-custom builders, with lot availability varying across established subdivisions and scattered parcels throughout the Lecanto corridor. A local specialist can identify which builders are currently active, which lots fit your goals, and how to compare custom build options versus available spec homes in this price range.
Is Lecanto close to Citrus County amenities?
Lecanto's central location gives it the shortest average drive time to any destination in Citrus County — Crystal River is roughly 20 minutes west, Inverness is 15 minutes east, Beverly Hills is 10 minutes south, and the Pine Ridge Equestrian community is just minutes away. Buyers prioritizing flexibility across the county's lifestyle amenities and employment centers will find Lecanto's geography hard to beat.
