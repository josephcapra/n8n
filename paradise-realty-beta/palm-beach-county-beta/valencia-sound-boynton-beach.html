About Valencia Sound

Boynton Beach · Palm Beach County, Florida

Age-Restricted Community (HOPA): Valencia Sound is certified under the Housing for Older Persons Act (HOPA). At least one resident per home must be 55 years of age or older. Please verify current age-restriction requirements with the community and your representative prior to purchase.

Valencia Sound is GL Homes' flagship 55+ community in Boynton Beach — widely regarded as one of the most amenity-rich active adult communities in all of Palm Beach County. As part of GL Homes' acclaimed Valencia brand, the community sets the standard for resort-style 55+ living in South Florida, combining a luxury clubhouse campus with vibrant social programming and a gated, low-maintenance lifestyle.

The HOA of approximately $450/month includes lawn care and access to the full amenity package — a compelling value for buyers seeking a maintenance-free Florida lifestyle without sacrificing quality. The resort pool and spa, tennis and pickleball courts, ballroom, and professional social events director create a community culture that rivals the best active adult communities in the country.

Boynton Beach's central location within Palm Beach County provides convenient access to beaches, dining, healthcare, and shopping — all within a short drive of the community gates.
