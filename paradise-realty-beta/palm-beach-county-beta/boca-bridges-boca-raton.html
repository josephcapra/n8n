About Boca Bridges

Boca Raton · Palm Beach County, Florida

Boca Bridges is GL Homes' most prestigious community in Palm Beach County — and by most measures, one of the most sought-after new luxury developments in all of South Florida. Situated in the heart of Boca Raton, the community delivers estate-size homes with exceptional construction quality, a $50+ million amenity package, and the cachet of a Boca Raton address behind 24-hour guard-gated security.

The amenity package at Boca Bridges is extraordinary even by South Florida standards: a 5-star clubhouse with resort pools, full-service spa, café and restaurant, tennis and pickleball courts, basketball, fitness center, game room, kids' club, and a dedicated social director. The scale and quality of this amenity campus sets Boca Bridges apart from virtually any other new construction gated community in the region.

GL Homes' construction quality and design studio program allow buyers to personalize their homes extensively — elevations, finishes, cabinetry, flooring, and structural options can be tailored through the design studio process before construction begins, delivering a semi-custom result with a production builder warranty and timeline.
