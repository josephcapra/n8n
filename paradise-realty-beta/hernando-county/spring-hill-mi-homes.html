About M/I Homes in Spring Hill

Spring Hill · Hernando County, Florida

M/I Homes (NYSE: MHO) is a publicly traded national homebuilder with a strong Florida presence, bringing its quality construction standards and 10-year structural warranty to Spring Hill's growing new construction market. M/I's Spring Hill communities deliver single-family homes starting from the low $300s with floor plans that balance square footage, functional layout, and the design studio personalization that buyers expect from a national production builder — while offering one of the strongest warranty programs in the industry.

M/I Homes is distinguished among national builders by the 10-year structural warranty it provides on every home — a program that covers the structural components of the home against material defects for a full decade after closing. This warranty is meaningful in Florida's climate, where foundation movement, moisture infiltration, and structural stress from hurricane wind loads create real risks that a comprehensive builder warranty helps protect against. For buyers choosing between national builders at comparable price points, M/I's warranty program is a differentiator worth factoring into the decision.

M/I Financial, the builder's in-house mortgage company, often provides incentive financing — rate buydowns or closing cost contributions — to buyers who use M/I Financial's preferred lending. A local specialist can help you evaluate whether M/I Financial's incentive offer represents genuine value or whether an independent lender produces a better overall outcome for your specific financial profile.
