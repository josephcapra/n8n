About Spring Hill Classic by Century Communities

Spring Hill · Hernando County, Florida

Spring Hill Classic is Century Communities' new construction community in Spring Hill, delivering efficiently designed single-family homes from the mid $300s with no CDD fees — a combination that makes the total monthly housing cost lower than many comparable-priced communities in the greater Tampa Bay market. Century Communities is one of the nation's top 10 homebuilders by volume, bringing production-builder efficiency and standardized quality to Spring Hill's active new construction market.

The no-CDD structure at Spring Hill Classic is meaningful for total-cost-of-ownership calculations. CDD fees in Pasco and Hillsborough County planned communities typically add $800–$3,000 per year to housing costs — costs that don't disappear when the CDD bond is paid off (management fees continue) and that can be significant over a 10–15 year horizon. For buyers who prioritize minimizing recurring costs, Spring Hill Classic's no-CDD structure is a genuine advantage over many competing communities.

Century Communities' "Classic" line emphasizes efficient floor plans and modern exterior designs with functional interiors at attainable price points. The community serves buyers who want new construction quality and the peace of mind of a national builder warranty without the premium pricing of more heavily amenitized communities — a practical choice for buyers prioritizing monthly payment and long-term cost over community pool access.
