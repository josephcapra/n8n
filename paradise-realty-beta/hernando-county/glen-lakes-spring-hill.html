About Glen Lakes in Spring Hill

Spring Hill · Hernando County, Florida

Glen Lakes is one of Hernando County's established golf communities, where Richmond American Homes is building new single-family homes from the mid $400s in a resort-like setting. The community's golf course creates a distinct lifestyle that separates Glen Lakes from Spring Hill's more conventional new construction neighborhoods — buyers here are often specifically seeking the amenity infrastructure, landscaping, and social character that golf communities provide, in addition to the new construction product itself.

Richmond American Homes is a national builder with a strong Florida presence, known for offering buyers meaningful choices in design studio customization and floor plan variety at the mid-tier price point. At Glen Lakes, Richmond American's floor plan lineup emphasizes larger single-story and two-story layouts suited to the community's golf course setting — spacious owner's suites, three-car garage options, and covered lanai configurations that take advantage of the community's open lot views.

Glen Lakes' Spring Hill location puts residents within a short drive of Weeki Wachee Springs, Gulf river access via Bayonet Point, and the Suncoast Expressway for Tampa commuters. For buyers stepping up from the low-$300s Spring Hill communities, Glen Lakes offers a clear lifestyle upgrade — golf community character, larger floor plans, and Richmond American's warranty program at a mid-$400s price point that remains significantly more affordable than comparable golf communities in Hillsborough or Pasco Counties.
