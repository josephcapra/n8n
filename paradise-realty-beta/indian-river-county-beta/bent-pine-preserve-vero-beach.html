About Bent Pine Preserve

Vero Beach · Indian River County, Florida

Bent Pine Preserve by GHO Homes is Vero Beach's gated golf community offering new construction single-family homes adjacent to the Bent Pine Golf Club — an 18-hole championship course that defines the backdrop for daily life here. For buyers who want a golf-adjacent lifestyle alongside GHO's construction quality, Bent Pine Preserve is a standout option in Indian River County.

GHO Homes brings its signature approach to Bent Pine Preserve — generous homesites, concrete block construction, and a Tailor-Made customization program that allows buyers to personalize finishes and select structural options before construction begins. Homes are designed for the Florida climate with covered lanais, pool-ready lots, and open-concept living areas that take full advantage of the Florida indoor-outdoor lifestyle.

Located in the established west Vero Beach corridor, Bent Pine Preserve offers convenient proximity to I-95, downtown Vero Beach, Vero Beach beaches, and Cleveland Clinic Indian River Hospital — balancing the privacy of a gated golf community with everyday accessibility that busy buyers demand.
