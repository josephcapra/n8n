About Hampton Park — Classic Series

Vero Beach · Indian River County, Florida

Hampton Park Classic Series by Meritage Homes is one of the most sought-after new construction communities in Vero Beach. Offering gated living with energy-efficient, all-block construction at an accessible price point, Hampton Park Classic delivers the Florida lifestyle without the luxury price tag.

Six thoughtfully designed floorplans range from 1,695 to 2,503 square feet with 3 to 4 bedrooms and 2 to 3 bathrooms — ideal for buyers relocating from South Florida looking for more space and value in a lower-cost-of-living market.

Located in the west Vero corridor with easy access to I-95, residents are close to Vero Beach's renowned beaches, the Indian River Lagoon, McKee Botanical Garden, and downtown's Riverside Theatre and Ocean Drive. Cleveland Clinic Indian River Hospital is minutes away.

Meritage Homes builds some of the most energy-efficient homes in the nation — featuring spray foam insulation, impact glass windows, and smart home technology as standard. Expect significantly lower utility costs and a more comfortable living environment year-round.
