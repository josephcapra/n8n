About Seaglass

Vero Beach Barrier Island · Indian River County, Florida

Seaglass by GHO Homes is the most distinctive luxury new construction community on Vero Beach's barrier island — an intimate enclave of just 72 homes where every residence includes a private screened pool as standard, and where the majestic canopy of preserved giant oak trees creates an atmosphere of natural grandeur that no other new community can replicate.

Located directly on A1A across from Disney's Vero Beach Resort, Seaglass occupies one of the most prestigious parcels in Indian River County. GHO went to extraordinary lengths to preserve the mature oaks that have stood on this site for decades — winding nature trails and charming boardwalks thread through the ancient canopy, creating a community that feels as much like a nature preserve as a neighborhood.

The exclusive resident Beach Club provides privileged beach access without the full oceanfront price tag. The clubhouse features a fitness center, community room, and outdoor seating with beach chairs available daily. Nine floorplans range from 2,254 to over 3,800 square feet — all customizable through GHO's Tailor-Made Program.

Natural gas connections, premium finishes, and impact glass throughout complete the picture. For buyers seeking authentic Old Florida character combined with new construction quality and luxury appointments, Seaglass stands alone in the Indian River County market.
