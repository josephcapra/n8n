About Waterway Village 55+ Active Adult

Vero Beach · Indian River County, Florida

Age-Qualified Community (55+) — At least one resident per home must be 55 years of age or older. This is an age-restricted community under the Housing for Older Persons Act (HOPA).

Waterway Village by DiVosta Homes is Indian River County's premier 55+ active adult new construction community — offering DiVosta's elevated design standards, a full amenity campus, and the gated security that buyers in age-qualified communities expect. DiVosta is the premier active adult brand within the PulteGroup family, with decades of experience building communities that become vibrant, sought-after neighborhoods over time.

Waterway Village offers both carriage homes (attached single-level residences) and single-family detached homes — giving buyers a range of ownership styles within the age-qualified neighborhood. DiVosta's Signature finishes are standard throughout: built-in kitchen packages, large-format tile, and premium standard features that bring polished design to every home without requiring extensive upgrades.

The amenity campus is the heart of Waterway Village life — a resort-style pool, fitness center with modern equipment, pickleball courts, and a full social calendar keep residents active, connected, and engaged. Vero Beach's world-class healthcare at Cleveland Clinic Indian River Hospital, cultural institutions, and beautiful beaches make this an exceptional setting for the 55+ lifestyle.
