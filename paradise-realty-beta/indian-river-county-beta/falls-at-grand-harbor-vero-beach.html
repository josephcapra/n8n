About The Falls at Grand Harbor

Grand Harbor · Vero Beach · Indian River County, Florida

The Falls at Grand Harbor by GHO Homes is a gated new construction community nestled within the prestigious Grand Harbor planned community on the Indian River Lagoon in Vero Beach. Grand Harbor is one of Indian River County's most established and desirable master-planned communities — featuring a waterfront clubhouse, marina, golf courses, and an active social lifestyle that has attracted residents for decades.

The Falls represents the new construction opportunity within this established setting — GHO Homes' single-family homes bringing updated design, energy efficiency, and modern finishes to a community whose infrastructure and amenities are already fully realized. Buyers choosing The Falls get both the GHO new construction experience and immediate access to Grand Harbor's complete resort lifestyle.

Generous homesites, GHO's signature concrete block construction, covered lanais, and pool-ready lots give buyers the canvas to create the Florida lifestyle they envision — with the backing of Grand Harbor's full amenity campus and waterfront location on the Indian River Lagoon.

Builder Incentives Available

GHO Homes offers Tailor-Made customization and periodic incentive programs at The Falls. Contact Paradise Realty FLA for current availability and any active programs before visiting the sales center.

Important: Grand Harbor has its own HOA structure alongside GHO's community HOA — we'll walk you through both before you sign anything.
