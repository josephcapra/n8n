About Verano at PGA Village

Port St. Lucie · St. Lucie County, Florida

Age-Qualified Community (55+) — At least one resident per home must be 55 years of age or older. This is an age-restricted community under the Housing for Older Persons Act (HOPA).

Verano at PGA Village is one of the largest and most celebrated 55+ active adult communities on Florida's Treasure Coast. Developed by GL Homes and set directly adjacent to the renowned PGA Village golf complex in Port St. Lucie, Verano offers an unmatched combination of low-maintenance living, world-class resort amenities, and a vibrant social calendar that keeps residents as active as they choose to be. With over 3,500 homes at full buildout, the scale of the community supports a full-service lifestyle unlike anything else in the region.

GL Homes builds single family homes and villas at Verano across a range of floorplans, giving buyers the flexibility to right-size without sacrificing quality. Interiors feature open-concept layouts, tile flooring in common areas, quartz or granite countertops depending on the series, stainless appliances, and impact-resistant windows and doors — a meaningful advantage in coastal Florida where hurricane protection is a real consideration. Covered lanais extend the living space outdoors, and many homesites offer lake or preserve views throughout the community's thoughtfully landscaped grounds.

The HOA fee of approximately $430 per month covers lawn maintenance, irrigation, exterior pest control, and full access to the Verano amenity campus — meaning residents effectively trade yard work for resort living. The master-gated entry provides a layer of security and privacy that many active adults prioritize, and the professional on-site management team ensures the community is consistently well-maintained. For buyers coming from out of state, the combination of turnkey maintenance and professional oversight makes the transition to Florida living significantly smoother.

Location adds another dimension of value. Verano sits within minutes of shopping, dining, and medical services in Port St. Lucie, and the proximity to PGA Village — home to multiple championship golf courses — is a genuine differentiator for golf enthusiasts. I-95 access connects residents to both Palm Beach and the greater Treasure Coast within a short drive. Whether you are relocating from the Northeast, downsizing from a larger family home, or simply seeking a community built around active adult living, Verano at PGA Village consistently ranks among the Treasure Coast's top choices.

Why Choose Verano at PGA Village?

Key reasons active adult buyers are choosing this community on the Treasure Coast

Golf at Your Doorstep

Verano sits directly adjacent to PGA Village, one of Florida's most celebrated golf destinations. Residents enjoy immediate access to championship-caliber play without leaving the neighborhood corridor.

True Resort Amenity Package

The Verano amenity campus goes well beyond a typical clubhouse — lagoon pool, resistance pool, tennis, pickleball, bocce, billiards, fitness center, and a full social director managing a packed events calendar year-round.

Genuinely Low-Maintenance Living

HOA fees cover lawn care, irrigation, and exterior pest control. The Verano lifestyle is designed to give residents their time back — no yard work, no maintenance headaches, just the activities and social life you moved to Florida for.

GL Homes Build Quality

GL Homes is one of South Florida's most established private builders. Verano homes feature impact windows and doors, quality finishes, and open-concept layouts designed for the way active adults actually live.

Treasure Coast Location

Port St. Lucie consistently ranks among Florida's fastest-growing cities. Verano residents are minutes from shopping, dining, beaches on Hutchinson Island, and healthcare facilities — with I-95 providing quick access to Palm Beach.

Location & Area

Nearby: PGA Village Golf Complex, Tradition Town Center (retail, dining, Cleveland Clinic Tradition Hospital), St. Lucie West commercial corridor, Hutchinson Island beaches, and I-95 interchange. Major grocery and retail anchors are within a 5–10 minute drive.

School District: St. Lucie County Public Schools — verify current zoning by specific lot address. Note: as a HOPA 55+ community, Verano is age-restricted and not primarily a consideration for school-district selection.

Port St. Lucie is located in St. Lucie County on Florida's Treasure Coast, roughly 50 miles north of West Palm Beach and 120 miles south of Orlando. The area offers a notably lower cost of living than comparable coastal South Florida markets while retaining easy access to international airports in West Palm Beach and Fort Lauderdale.
