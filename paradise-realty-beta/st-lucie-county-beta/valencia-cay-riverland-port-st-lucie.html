About Valencia Cay at Riverland

Port St. Lucie · St. Lucie County, Florida

Age-Qualified Community (55+) — At least one resident per home must be 55 years of age or older. This is an age-restricted community under the Housing for Older Persons Act (HOPA).

Valencia Cay at Riverland is GL Homes' flagship 55+ active adult community within the Riverland master-planned development in Port St. Lucie — and it sets a standard for amenity-rich living that is genuinely difficult to match on the Treasure Coast. Valencia Cay is the age-qualified village within Riverland, meaning residents enjoy the exclusivity of a HOPA 55+ environment while simultaneously having access to the full Riverland amenity campus, which spans the entire master community. This dual-access model — private village amenities plus shared master-community amenities — is rare and represents exceptional value for the HOA investment.

Single family homes at Valencia Cay range from approximately 1,600 to 3,000 square feet across a thoughtful spectrum of floorplans, allowing buyers to choose a footprint that matches their actual lifestyle rather than settling for what is available. GL Homes equips these homes with open-concept great rooms, generous kitchen islands, walk-in closets, split bedroom arrangements for privacy, and impact-resistant windows and doors as standard — not as costly upgrades. Covered lanais and lake-view homesites are plentiful, reinforcing the sense that the outdoor lifestyle is central to the Valencia Cay experience.

The Riverland master amenity campus is what truly distinguishes Valencia Cay from competing 55+ communities. In addition to Valencia Cay's own resort pool with lap lanes and luxury clubhouse, Riverland residents have access to a full performing arts and cultural center — an extraordinary feature for any residential community and exceedingly rare in an active adult setting. The Paseo is a multi-mile fitness and walking trail that winds through the master community, and Riverland's Adventure Park adds recreational variety that appeals to a wide range of activity levels and interests.

The HOA of approximately $460 per month reflects the breadth of what is included: lawn care, irrigation, community maintenance, and full access to both Valencia Cay's own amenities and Riverland's master campus. For buyers evaluating true cost of living, the included lawn maintenance alone offsets a substantial portion of the fee compared to maintaining a private home — and that calculation doesn't account for the social, fitness, and cultural programming that residents describe as transformative to their daily lives. For buyers ready to make a serious move to active adult living on Florida's Treasure Coast, Valencia Cay at Riverland is a top-tier consideration.

Why Choose Valencia Cay at Riverland?

Key reasons active adult buyers are choosing this community in Port St. Lucie

Performing Arts Center — A True Rarity

Riverland's performing arts and cultural center is an amenity virtually unheard of in residential communities. Valencia Cay residents have full access, making cultural programming part of everyday life — not a special outing.

Dual-Level Amenity Access

Valencia Cay residents enjoy both their own private 55+ clubhouse and resort pool and the full Riverland master campus — effectively double the amenities of a standard single-community HOA at a comparable price point.

The Paseo Fitness Trail

Riverland's Paseo is a dedicated multi-mile walking, jogging, and cycling trail that winds through the master community — a beloved daily amenity that residents cite as one of the most-used features of the entire development.

Flexible Floorplan Range

With homes from 1,600 to 3,000 square feet and 2 to 4 bedrooms, Valencia Cay accommodates everything from right-sizing empty nesters to buyers who want a guest suite or dedicated home office without compromise.

Master-Planned with Long-Term Vision

Riverland is one of Port St. Lucie's most deliberately planned communities, with infrastructure, amenities, and landscaping designed to hold value. Buyers are investing in a well-capitalized development with clear long-term stewardship.

Location & Area

Nearby: Riverland is located in the western Port St. Lucie corridor with convenient access to Tradition Town Center (retail, dining, Cleveland Clinic Tradition Hospital), Gatlin Boulevard commercial district, and I-95. Hutchinson Island beaches are approximately 25–30 minutes east.

School District: St. Lucie County Public Schools — verify current zoning by specific lot address. Note: as a HOPA 55+ community, Valencia Cay is age-restricted and not primarily a consideration for school-district selection.

Port St. Lucie is positioned on Florida's Treasure Coast between Palm Beach County to the south and Martin County to the north. The city has invested significantly in infrastructure and recreational facilities over the past decade, and its growth trajectory makes it one of the more compelling markets on Florida's east coast for active adult buyers seeking long-term value.
