About Marina Del Palma

Palm Coast · Flagler County, Florida

Marina Del Palma is Palm Coast's signature luxury marina community — a gated Intracoastal Waterway development by ICI Homes that offers what virtually no other Flagler County new construction community can: direct boating access with private marina slip availability from your home community. For buyers whose lifestyle revolves around the water, Marina Del Palma removes the most common friction of Florida boating life — the distance between home and boat.

ICI Homes built Marina Del Palma with a clear design philosophy: luxury construction quality, private marina infrastructure, and a gated setting that provides security and exclusivity without the corporate homogeneity of volume builders. Homes from the high $400s are positioned on the Intracoastal with views of the waterway, and the community's private marina gives residents boat slip access that would otherwise require a separate marina membership or dry storage arrangement.

Palm Coast's ICW position gives Marina Del Palma residents access to one of Florida's most scenic coastal waterway systems — the Intracoastal connecting south toward Ponce Inlet, New Smyrna, and the Indian River Lagoon, and north toward St. Augustine, the Matanzas River, and Jacksonville. For serious boaters, this geography puts the Atlantic Ocean within a reasonable cruising distance in both directions.

Builder Incentives & Current Availability

Marina Del Palma availability is limited by the community's finite ICW footprint and ICI Homes' build pace. Marina slip availability is separate from home purchase and subject to its own waitlist and pricing. Our Flagler County specialist can provide current information on which lots remain, what slip availability looks like, and how to navigate the purchase process for both the home and the marina component simultaneously.
