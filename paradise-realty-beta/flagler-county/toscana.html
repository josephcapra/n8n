About Toscana

Palm Coast · Flagler County, Florida

Toscana is Palm Coast's Mediterranean-inspired gated community — a departure from the coastal and golf community typology that defines most of Flagler County's luxury new construction. ICI Homes brought a distinct architectural vision to Toscana: barrel tile roofs, arched entries, warm stucco finishes, and Mediterranean landscaping that create a cohesive aesthetic identity unlike anything else in Palm Coast's residential market.

The gated format provides security and community exclusivity while the ICI Homes construction standard delivers quality finishes, thoughtful floor plan design, and the kind of craftsmanship that distinguishes Palm Coast's mid $400s luxury tier from its mainstream market. Toscana buyers are choosing a community defined by its visual character as much as its amenities — the Mediterranean architecture is not a facade element but a design commitment carried through every home in the community.

Palm Coast's broader infrastructure surrounds Toscana — AdventHealth's hospital network, the 125+ mile trail system, A-rated schools, and the Town Center commercial district are all accessible within 10–15 minutes. The community's gated position within this infrastructure network gives residents the exclusivity of a private community with the convenience of a fully developed urban context.

What is Toscana in Palm Coast?

The community features barrel tile roofs, arched entries, and Mediterranean architectural character that distinguishes it from Palm Coast's coastal and golf community typology.
Is Toscana in Palm Coast gated?
Yes. Toscana is a gated community with controlled access — one of Palm Coast's smaller gated communities alongside Grand Haven, Palm Coast Plantation, and Marina Del Palma. The gated format combined with ICI Homes' Mediterranean design standard creates a community identity distinct from Palm Coast's open-plan neighborhoods.

ICI Homes is the primary builder at Toscana. ICI Homes is a Florida-based luxury builder with decades of experience in Flagler County, known for quality construction, design flexibility, and a finish standard that distinguishes the brand from volume builders at lower price tiers.
