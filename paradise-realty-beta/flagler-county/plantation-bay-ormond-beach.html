About Plantation Bay

Ormond Beach · Flagler County, Florida

Discover Plantation Bay, a private sanctuary in the charming seaside town of Ormond Beach, Florida. Behind the 24/7 manned gates of this ICI Homes flagship community, you’ll find a harmonious blend of convenience and tranquility, with easy access to shopping, dining, and historic St. Augustine. Set amid acres of natural beauty, Plantation Bay offers a lifestyle like no other. Indulge in world-class amenities, including the 40,500-square-foot Founders Club Golf and Lifestyle Center, the Prestwick Clubhouse, a 7,000-square-foot Spa and Fitness Center, and two-and-a-half championship golf courses. Add lighted Har-Tru tennis and pickleball courts, scenic trails, and parks, and you have the ultimate playground for all ages. What truly sets Plantation Bay apart is its customization options, a signature of ICI Homes. With over 44 years of experience, ICI is renowned for designing homes “built around your imagination.” From altering floor plans to creating one-of-a-kind spaces, our team ensures your home reflects your unique lifestyle. Here, you can opt into the amenities you love without paying for those you don’t. Whether enjoying our swimming pools, playgrounds, or community events, Plantation Bay invites you to live life on your terms. Welcome to a community where innovation meets elegance, and every detail is crafted to inspire—a hallmark of ICI Homes’ dedication to enhancing lives and building legacies.
