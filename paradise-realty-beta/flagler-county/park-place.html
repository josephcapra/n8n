About Park Place

Palm Coast · Flagler County, Florida

Park Place is an established Palm Coast community where new construction and infill building continue to add inventory to a neighborhood with mature infrastructure, tree canopy, and the kind of neighborhood character that master-planned communities need decades to develop. For buyers in the mid $300s who want Palm Coast's full infrastructure — 125+ miles of trails, A-rated schools, AdventHealth hospital, and established commercial districts — without the feel of a brand-new subdivision, Park Place offers a grounded alternative.

The Palm Coast trail system connects Park Place residents to the broader city trail network — one of the largest paved trail systems in Florida — giving cyclists, walkers, and runners direct access to parks, the Intracoastal Waterway, and other neighborhoods without using roadways. This connectivity is one of Palm Coast's most underappreciated lifestyle features and is fully accessible from Park Place's established street grid.

Buyers who choose Park Place over Grand Landings or newer master-planned communities are typically prioritizing neighborhood maturity over resort amenities — they want a community that already feels settled, with canopy trees, established neighbors, and a streetscape that doesn't look like it was installed last year. In a city as actively growing as Palm Coast, that quality is increasingly meaningful.
