About Beverly Beach New Construction

Beverly Beach · Flagler County, Florida

Beverly Beach is one of Florida's smallest incorporated municipalities — a narrow oceanfront community of roughly 600 residents hugging the A1A corridor between Marineland to the north and Flagler Beach to the south. New construction here is primarily custom and infill in nature: the land supply is genuinely limited, ocean-to-river lots are the dominant footprint, and buyers who secure new construction in Beverly Beach are buying into one of Flagler County's most constrained and distinctive coastal addresses.

Homes from the mid $300s occupy lots where the Atlantic Ocean is often visible from the front door and the Matanzas River or Intracoastal Waterway is steps from the back. This is not a master-planned amenity community — it is a small coastal town where the lifestyle is the amenity. Washington Oaks Gardens State Park sits immediately to the north, providing one of Florida's most photographed coquina rock beaches and formal gardens; the Marineland dolphin conservation facility is minutes away.

Buyers choosing Beverly Beach are typically trading community infrastructure for coastal authenticity. There is no resort pool or fitness center — there is the Atlantic Ocean, the river, the A1A scenic highway, and a quiet town that doesn't try to be anything other than what it is. For the right buyer, that trade is not a compromise at all.

Why Choose Beverly Beach New Construction?

Key reasons buyers are choosing this Flagler County community

Genuinely Constrained Coastal Inventory

Beverly Beach has no room to expand. The land between the ocean and the river is finite, the town is already incorporated, and new construction here is true infill in a community that hasn't grown significantly in decades. When new construction becomes available, it moves quickly — because buyers who've been watching know it won't come back.

Ocean-to-River Lifestyle

Beverly Beach lots frequently offer Atlantic views to the east and Matanzas River or Intracoastal access to the west. The lifestyle is defined by both corridors — morning beach walks, afternoon paddling or fishing, and sunsets over the river are the daily rhythm for residents who choose this address over Palm Coast's more amenity-rich but inland communities.

Washington Oaks Gardens at Your Doorstep

Washington Oaks Gardens State Park — home to Florida's iconic coquina rock beach and formal gardens — is immediately north of Beverly Beach. Residents have direct trail and beach access to one of Flagler County's most beautiful and least crowded state park destinations, without the drive that visitors make from Palm Coast or Flagler Beach.

Location & Area

Nearby: Marineland (~3 min north), Washington Oaks Gardens State Park (~5 min), Flagler Beach (~12 min south), Palm Coast (~20 min south), St. Augustine (~35 min north).

Flagler County Context: Beverly Beach sits at the northern tip of Flagler County on the A1A coastal highway — geographically closer to St. Augustine than to Palm Coast, but within Flagler County's boundaries. The drive to St. Augustine's historic district takes about 35 minutes; Palm Coast's main retail and healthcare infrastructure is 20 minutes south. Buyers who value coastal character over commercial convenience find Beverly Beach's position genuinely unique.

Commute Patterns: Beverly Beach is best suited for remote workers, retirees, or buyers whose employment is in St. Augustine. The A1A route south to Palm Coast is scenic but slower than I-95; buyers commuting to Daytona Beach or Ormond Beach have a 40–50 minute drive that's beautiful but long. I-95 access requires a drive west to US-1 or State Road 100 first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Beverly Beach New Construction and buying in Flagler County

What is Beverly Beach in Flagler County?
Beverly Beach is a small incorporated oceanfront community on A1A in northern Flagler County, FL — situated between Marineland and Flagler Beach with direct Atlantic Ocean and Matanzas River access. New construction here is custom and infill in nature, with homes from the mid $300s in one of Flagler County's most constrained and distinctive coastal markets.
Is Beverly Beach a good place to live in Florida?
Beverly Beach offers something genuinely rare — a quiet oceanfront community of about 600 residents where the ocean and river are part of daily life, not a weekend destination. Washington Oaks Gardens State Park is adjacent to the north. For buyers prioritizing coastal authenticity over community amenities, Beverly Beach is one of Florida's best-kept addresses at its price point.
How far is Beverly Beach from St. Augustine?
Beverly Beach is approximately 35 minutes from historic downtown St. Augustine via A1A north or US-1. The drive is scenic along the coastal highway. Buyers who work or spend significant time in St. Augustine will find Beverly Beach's northern Flagler County position more convenient than Palm Coast, despite technically being in Flagler County.
What is the inventory like for new construction in Beverly Beach?
New construction inventory in Beverly Beach is very limited by the town's geography and size. Custom infill and lot-by-lot builds are the primary path. A local specialist familiar with the Beverly Beach market is the most effective way to identify what's buildable before it reaches broader public listing.
